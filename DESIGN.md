## Brand & Style

This design system translates the heavy-duty scale of industrial electrical contracting and power engineering into a precision-engineered digital interface. The visual direction balances the dependability of Canadian infrastructure-grade execution with the surgical crispness of high-tech hardware interfaces. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Surgical Reliability:** Evoking absolute zero-downtime trust, certified compliance (Red Seal, ESA, CSA), and computational precision.
- **High-Tech Industrial:** Avoiding the worn, utilitarian grit of generic field services in favor of an architectural, cleanroom-grade power systems aesthetic.
- **Authoritative & Dynamic:** Grounded in monumental graphite and slate foundations, charged by purposeful energetic accents that signal active current, load balance, and technical vigilance.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Expression
The system adopts an **Engineered Industrial Modernism** paradigm:
- **Foundational Surfaces:** Deep slate and monolithic graphite tiers replace standard web neutrals.
- **Current Accents:** Controlled applications of energized volt amber and high-voltage cyan indicate state, status, safety ratings, and mission-critical actions.
- **Micro-Structured Layouts:** Hairline borders (1px with calibrated alpha values), technical data badges, mono-spaced telemetry readouts, and disciplined structural framing.
- **National Trust Marks:** Subtle, monochrome-treated Canadian Maple accents and institutional verification insignia integrated without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high-contrast legibility in field, control room, and corporate procurement environments. A dominant dark architecture establishes weight and authority, punctured by high-luminescence technical accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#F59E0B` - Volt Amber):** Used for critical conversion targets, emergency service triggers, energized status indicators, and primary points of user interaction. Reflects active electrical hazard standards and industrial illumination.
- **Secondary (`#0EA5E9` - Safety Cyan / Arc Blue):** Used for technical telemetry, verification seals, active inspection phases, engineering diagrams, and structural navigation accents.
- **Tertiary (`#EAB308` - Kinetic Gold):** Applied selectively for certified accreditation badges (Red Seal, Master Electrician), warranty tiers, and rating highlights.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Monolith Slate):** The bedrock of the interface. Sub-surfaces transition through calibrated slate-navy tiers (`#0B0F19`, `#1E293B`, `#334155`) to establish depth without true-black optical fatigue.
- **Signal White (`#F8FAFC`):** Primary text and vital schematic graphics, ensuring maximum luminance contrast ratios exceeding WCAG AAA requirements against dark structural containers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances modern architectural clarity with technical instrumentation precision:

- **Editorial & Structural Typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, geometric grotesque shapes with wide apertures ensure sharp readability at large scales and effortless scanning in dense content sections. Tightly tracked display and headline styles communicate industrial solidity.
- **Instrumentation & Telemetry Typography (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied exclusively to metadata, certification IDs (e.g., `ESA #7004128`), spec tables, diagnostic readouts, step indicators, and high-trust certification badges.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Text blocks must never exceed 68 characters per line to maintain scanning velocity for project engineers, safety auditors, and facility managers.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses a strictly disciplined 12-column grid built on an 8pt base grid rhythm, evoking structural engineering blueprints and industrial automation panels.

### Layout Rules
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `gutter: 1.5rem` and `margin-desktop: 4rem`. Major engineering service matrices and equipment status overviews follow asymmetrical 8/4 or 4/4/4 module splits.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `gutter: 1.5rem` and `margin: 2rem`. Multicolumn grids collapse to 4/4 balanced vertical pairings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `gutter-mobile: 1rem` and `margin-mobile: 1rem`. Dynamic stack layout ensuring thumb-zone accessibility for high-priority dispatch and emergency contact pathways.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Section transitions utilize `space-3xl` for high-impact content separation, while internal card elements remain tightly clustered using `space-xs` through `space-md` to preserve technical cohesion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic heavy drop shadows. Instead, it relies on high-tech surface luminance, calibrated micro-borders, and subtle backlit glow effects that simulate illuminated control equipment.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Base Layer (Ground Zero - `#0B0F19`):** The non-reflective structural canvas hosting background gridlines and canvas-level engineering patterns.
2. **Surface Tier 1 (`#0F172A` with `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Base cards, data tables, and standard content sections.
3. **Surface Tier 2 (Frosted Micro-Glass - `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`):** Interactive overlays, sticky navigation bars, floating dispatch trackers, and critical status cards. Micro-borders shift to `1px solid rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.2)` on active focus.
4. **Active/Energized Elevation (Electrified Glow):** For interactive focal points and emergency service prompts, cards cast a diffused colored aura:
   - Primary Action Glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`
   - Technical Audit Glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.25)`
5. **Hairline Separators:** All horizontal dividers and internal cell grids strictly use 1px width with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` or `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.15)` for technical schematics.

## Shapes

The interface balances heavy-duty industrial authority with modern aerodynamic refinement. Standard structural components utilize a disciplined 0.5rem base radius (`roundedness: 2`), expanding to 1rem (`rounded-lg`) and 1.5rem (`rounded-xl`) for elevated container assemblies.

### Shape Guidelines
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Enclosed within `rounded-xl` envelopes (1.5rem). The smooth corners soften dense engineering data matrices while preserving an architectural structure.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs, & Segment Controls:** Styled with `rounded-md` (0.5rem) to evoke precision-milled hardware switches.
- **Badges, Tags, & Telemetry Chips:** Border-radius capped at `0.25rem` or full-pill configuration depending on utility. Technical spec chips stay semi-sharp (0.25rem) to reinforce monospaced data parameters, while trust verification tags use full pill borders.
- **Accents:** Precision diagonal chamfers or 45-degree micro-notches may be used on engineering drawing cards and technical inspection printouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Dispatch / Request Quote"):** High-visibility Volt Amber background (`#F59E0B`), text in Obsidian (`#0F172A`, 700 weight), with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On hover, transitions to `#D97706` with an amber ambient field glow.
- **Secondary ("Engineering Specs / Schematics"):** Frosted slate container (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`), border in Arc Blue (`rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.4)`), text in Pure White (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Emergency Button:** High-contrast hazard perimeter with diagonal warning micro-hatching and pulsing red-amber status light.

### Cards & Container Panels
- Engineered with `rounded-xl` geometry, deep slate gradient fills (`linear-gradient(180deg, #1E293B 0%, #0F172A 100%)`), and 1px hairline perimeter borders.
- Internal padding matches `space-lg` (desktop) and `space-md` (mobile).
- Micro-frosted variants utilize 12px blur with semi-transparent navy substrates.

### Badges & Trust Seals (Red Seal, ESA, CSA, Canadian Standards)
- **Compliance Badge:** Inline pill or compact rectangular block framed in a 1px hairline border with a dedicated status LED indicator (green: active/compliant, amber: scheduled review).
- **Canadian Origin Mark:** De-saturated high-precision maple leaf emblem with subtle volt or cyan accent, integrated into the header navigation and site verification footer alongside license registration numbers.
- **Typography:** Rendered strictly in `label-sm` or `label-md` using JetBrains Mono with uppercase tracking.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Dark recessed base (`#0B0F19`), 1px structural border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), text in `#F8FAFC`. Focus states transition the border to Cyan (`#0EA5E9`) with an internal micro-glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom square/circular mechanical toggles with crisp 1px borders. Checked state snaps to Volt Amber with a sharp technical checkmark glyph.

### Data Lists & Telemetry Tables
- Alternating subtle rows (`#0F172A` / `#131D31`) separated by hairline dividers.
- Metrics and specifications rendered in JetBrains Mono with right-aligned values and color-coded status chips (Active Load, Phase Balanced, Scheduled Overhaul).
